WordClash

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

WordClash is an offline, single-player crossword game. No account is required. Game scores stay on your device. Advertisements use Google AdMob and require an internet connection.

About this policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use WordClash. For privacy questions, contact the WordClash developer at alphagamesgg77@gmail.com.

Game information stored on your device

WordClash stores best scores, level progress, and an advertising age group locally on your device. The app has no multiplayer service, player-account system, or game server, and the developer does not receive your game scores.

You can erase locally stored game information by clearing WordClash's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Clearing storage also resets your saved progress.

Advertisements and information handled by Google

WordClash uses Google AdMob to display banner, rewarded, and interstitial advertisements. Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device and other identifiers used by the advertising SDK. WordClash removes the Android advertising-ID permission for all players.
  • App interactions and advertising activity, such as ad views and clicks.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs and related technical data.

This information may be processed for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service operation. Advertising may be personalized where permitted and subject to your choices. Google's handling and retention of this information are described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

The game can be played offline. Advertisements and online consent messages require an internet connection. Optional rewarded ads grant an extra letter hint only after the advertising SDK confirms that the reward was earned.

Consent and your choices

Where required, WordClash uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present privacy and consent choices before requesting advertisements. Players under 18 and players whose age is unknown are tagged as under the age of consent. When the consent system requires a privacy-options entry point, an Ad privacy choices button appears on the home screen so you can revisit your choices.

You can also manage advertising preferences through your Android device settings and Google account. Available options depend on your region, device, and Google's services.

Children and age screening

WordClash is intended for both children and older players. On first launch, a neutral age question is shown before the app initializes advertising. Your exact entered age is not saved or sent to the developer or Google. Only an adult (18 or older) or protected advertising group is saved on your device. Choosing Prefer not to say uses the protected group.

For players under 18 and players of unknown age, the app requests child age treatment and non-personalized ads through Google AdMob, limits ad content to Google's G category, and disables automatic interstitial ads. All players can play the same puzzles. Banner ads are separate from game controls, and rewarded ads are optional. The app does not provide chat, accounts, purchases, or location tracking. It does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, or microphone access during gameplay.

Advertising age-treatment signals are supplied to Google; an exact age or date of birth is not supplied. Advertising services can still process technical data for contextual advertising, delivery, diagnostics, and fraud prevention under applicable restrictions. The app uses Google Mobile Ads without additional mediation adapters.

Support messages

If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. This information is used to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

Data access and deletion

Clear the app's storage to delete local game information. To ask about access to or deletion of information you sent directly to the developer, email alphagamesgg77@gmail.com. For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy controls and the resources linked above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or service providers change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
